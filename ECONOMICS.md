# ECONOMICS — SpendLens

## What a Converted Lead Is Worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI credits — the same Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise, and GitHub Copilot subscriptions that teams are already buying, at up to 60% off. A converted lead is a company that books a Credex consultation and purchases a credit bundle.

**Estimating deal size:**

- Target user: engineering team of 10 at a seed/Series A startup
- Typical AI spend before audit: $2,000–$5,000/month
- Credex discount: 30–60% off retail
- If the team buys 6 months of Claude Team + Cursor Pro through Credex:
  - Claude Team: 10 seats × $30 × 6 months = $1,800 retail → Credex sells at $1,080 (40% off), margin is ~25%
  - Cursor Business: 10 seats × $40 × 6 months = $2,400 retail → Credex sells at $1,440, margin ~25%
  - Total deal value: ~$2,500, Credex gross margin: ~$625

**Conservative estimate per closed deal: $400–$800 gross margin**
**Repeat purchase rate assumption: 40% rebuy within 12 months (second bundle)**
**LTV per converted customer: ~$800–$1,600 over 18 months**

## CAC at Each GTM Channel

| Channel | Estimated CAC | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Hacker News Show HN | $0 | Organic; no paid spend |

| Indie Hackers post | $0 | Organic |

At organic CAC of ~$0 and LTV of ~$800, the economics are extremely favorable — the tool pays back on the first converted deal regardless of how many audits run.

## Conversion Funnel

| Stage | Rate | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Visitor → audit completed | 15–25% | Typical free tool with no friction |
| Audit completed → email captured | 25–35% | Value is shown first; above-average for lead gen |
| Email captured → consultation booked | 10–20% | Only users with >$500/mo savings see the CTA |
| Consultation → credit purchase | 30–50% | Warm, intent-qualified lead who already did the math |

**End-to-end: 100 visitors → 20 audits → 6 emails → 1 consultation → 0.4 purchases**

This funnel requires ~250 visitors per closed deal. At 10,000 monthly visitors (achievable post-HN launch), that's 40 closed deals/month × $600 avg margin = **$24,000/month gross margin**.

## What Would Have to Be True for $1M ARR in 18 Months

$1M ARR = ~$83,000/month revenue to Credex.

At an average deal of $600 gross margin and 40% rebuy rate factored into LTV of $900:
- Need ~92 new closed deals/month sustained
- At 0.4% visitor → purchase, need ~23,000 monthly visitors by month 6
- At 1% conversion (optimized funnel): ~9,200 monthly visitors

**Path to $1M ARR:**
1. Months 1–3: Organic distribution (HN, Twitter, IH) → 2,000–5,000 monthly visitors → 10–20 closed deals/month → $6,000–$12,000/month
2. Months 4–9: Credex customer list activation + SEO from the pricing data content → 5,000–15,000 monthly visitors → 20–60 deals/month
3. Months 10–18: Word-of-mouth from shared audit URLs + Credex case studies → 15,000–25,000 monthly visitors + improved conversion from trust → 60–100 deals/month

**This is achievable if:** the audit engine is accurate enough that engineering managers trust it, the Credex consultation conversion rate stays above 30%, and at least one high-traffic distribution moment (HN front page, newsletter mention) occurs in the first 60 days.

**What kills it:** if the audit engine gives wrong recommendations (trust is destroyed), if Credex's sales cycle is too slow to convert consultations, or if AI tool pricing changes faster than the engine is updated.
