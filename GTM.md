# GTM — SpendLens

## Exact Target User

**Primary:** Engineering managers at seed-to-Series B startups (10–80 person companies) who own the engineering budget but don't have a finance team reviewing software spend. They have 4–12 developers, pay for AI tools on individual cards or a company card, and have never audited the stack. They signed up for ChatGPT Plus and Cursor Pro for each developer at different times and have never revisited whether those are still the right plans. Annual AI spend: $5,000–$60,000.

**Secondary:** Technical founders at pre-seed/seed startups who code alongside their team. They know what each tool does but haven't benchmarked their per-developer AI spend against alternatives.

**Not the target:** Enterprise procurement teams (they have vendors for this), solo developers spending less than $50/month (low savings potential), non-technical founders (won't understand the tool breakdown).

## First 100 Users in 30 Days with $0 Budget

**Week 1 — Hacker News Show HN**
Post "Show HN: SpendLens — free AI spend audit for engineering teams" on a Tuesday or Wednesday morning (9am ET). Target 50+ upvotes. HN readers are exactly the target user: technical, budget-conscious, skeptical of marketing fluff. The key is showing the actual audit output in the post — a real example with $400/mo in savings found.

**Week 2 — X/Twitter thread**
Write a thread: "I audited our AI tool spend and found we were paying for 3 overlapping tools. Here's what I found and what we switched to:" Include SpendLens as the tool at the end. Tag accounts in the developer-tools space who might retweet. One retweet from an account with 10k+ followers gets 20–50 sign-ups.

**Week 3 — Indie Hackers post**
Post a detailed breakdown: "How I built an AI spend audit tool in a week and what we learned about how startups waste money on AI." Include the full stack, real data, and a link. IH posts with genuine transparency perform well.

**Week 4 — Direct outreach**
Message 20 engineering managers on LinkedIn with a personalized note: "Hey [name], I built a free tool that audits AI tool spend — curious if the findings match what you're seeing at [company]. Would love your feedback." Don't pitch. Ask for feedback. Many will run the audit just to see.
