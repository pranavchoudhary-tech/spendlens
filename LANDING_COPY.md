## FAQ — 5 Real Q&As

**Q1: Is this actually free? What's the catch?**
A: SpendLens is free because it's built by Credex, a marketplace for discounted AI credits. If your audit shows more than $500/month in savings opportunity, we'll surface Credex as a way to capture even more. That's the pitch — transparent and only shown when relevant. The audit is useful either way.

**Q2: Do I need to create an account or log in?**
A: No. You enter your spend, get your results, and you're done. Email is only asked after you've already seen your savings number — and it's optional.

**Q3: How accurate is the pricing data?**
A: All pricing is pulled from official vendor pricing pages and verified against them weekly. Every number in the audit engine traces to a source URL with a verification date (see PRICING_DATA.md in the GitHub repo). We're transparent about methodology.

**Q4: What if my setup is already optimized?**
A: We'll tell you honestly. If your audit shows less than $100/month in savings, you'll see "You're spending well" with a genuine breakdown of why. We don't manufacture fake savings — that would undermine the entire point of the tool.

**Q5: Is my data private? Who sees my spend numbers?**
A: Your audit data is stored with a random ID, not linked to your name or company unless you choose to share your email. The shareable URL is public but contains only tool names and savings figures — no company name, no email, no seat counts that could identify you. You choose what to share.
